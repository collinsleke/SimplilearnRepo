# METSI Platform — Product Viewpoint

## Product Vision

> **Make water loss visible, recoverable, and investable — turning every drop of data into a decision, a revenue stream, or a public good.**

METSI is not a leak detection tool. It is not a dispatch system. It is not a billing engine.

METSI is a **water intelligence operating system** — a multi-sided platform that connects physical infrastructure (sensors, pipes, valves) with operational workflows (detect, dispatch, repair, verify) and financial markets (investors, contractors, municipalities). The output is **verified water savings**, and the by-product is an **exhaustive data asset** that grows more valuable with every leak repaired, every sensor reading recorded, and every revenue rand distributed.

---

## Product Positioning

### Current State (MVP)

The platform today is a **transactional backbone** — it digitises the end-to-end leak lifecycle:

- **Ingest** telemetry → **Detect** anomalies → **Score** leaks → **Dispatch** crews → **Verify** repairs → **Distribute** revenue
- 13 microservices, 6 DMN decision tables, 23 BPMN processes, 20 Kafka topics
- 34K+ time-series readings, 200+ seed records, 8 spatial tables across 6 DMA zones
- Multi-tenant by design (`X-Tenant-ID`), JWT auth, role-based dashboards for 5 stakeholder personas

**What it does well:** orchestrates a complex multi-stakeholder process from end to end with an immutable audit trail and transparent revenue flows.

**What it doesn't do yet:** turn the data flowing through that backbone into **insight, prediction, benchmarking, or new revenue**.

### Future State (Data Products Layer)

The platform evolves from **transactional backbone** to **intelligence layer** — where every operational process generates a reusable data product:

> **Phase 1 (MVP):** We connect the pipe. Data flows end-to-end.
>
> **Phase 2 (Analytics):** We surface what the data means. Dashboards, trends, alerts.
>
> **Phase 3 (Prediction):** We forecast what happens next. ML models, risk scores, recommendations.
>
> **Phase 4 (Marketplace):** We sell the insight. Data APIs, benchmarks, ESG reports, white-labelled analytics.

This document captures the full product viewpoint — what METSI is, who it serves, how it creates value, and what it will become.

## Data Product Portfolio

### Tier 1 — Operational Analytics (Build immediately, low effort, high value)

These products require **no ML, no new infrastructure** — just SQL aggregations + a charting library on the frontend.

| Product | Data Sources | Output | Delivery |
|---|---|---|---|
| **DMA Zone Performance** | telemetry.sensor_readings, detection.detected_leaks, twin.digital_twin_states | NRW%, avg pressure, avg flow, leak density, trend arrows per zone | Dashboard (Control Tower + Municipality) |
| **Crew Scorecard** | dispatch.jobs, mv.verification_records | SLA compliance %, avg response time, jobs/crew/month, first-time fix rate | Dashboard (Contractor + Dispatch) |
| **Sensor Health Monitor** | telemetry.sensor_heartbeats, telemetry.sensor_readings | Online %, data gap duration, last reading delta | Dashboard (Admin + Control Tower) |
| **SLA Compliance Tracker** | dispatch.jobs (eta vs sla_minutes), audit.audit_logs (SLA_VIOLATED) | % on-time, avg breach severity, trend by zone/crew/time-of-day | Dashboard (Municipality + Auditor) |
| **Leak Hotspot Map** | gis.mv_leaks, detection.detected_leaks, gis.mv_pipelines | Density heatmap, recurring leak zones, pipe material failure correlation | Map overlay (Control Tower + Leak Intelligence) |
| **Revenue Waterfall** | revenue.revenue_records, revenue.tariffs | Trended revenue per zone: volume → tariff → gross → stakeholder splits | Dashboard (Revenue + Investor) |
| **Asset Lifecycle Analytics** | gis.network_assets, gis mv_pipelines/valves/hydrants | Condition distribution, age profile, maintenance frequency by asset type | Dashboard (Asset Lifecycle) |

### Tier 2 — Predictive & ML Products (Build medium-term, medium effort, high differentiation)

These require a **data science workbench** or notebook-to-production pipeline.

| Product | Approach | Training Data | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Leak Risk Prediction** | Gradient-boosted model scoring pipes by failure probability | Pipe age, material, condition_score, historical leak density, pressure variance, soil type | Per-pipe risk score (0–100), zone-level risk heatmap |
| **Seasonal NRW Forecasting** | Time-series model (Prophet / ARIMA) per DMA zone | 12+ months of sensor_readings, weather metadata, calendar features | Expected NRW% by month, anomaly flags for unexpected spikes |
| **Dynamic Leak Thresholds** | Unsupervised anomaly detection (isolation forest / autoencoder) learning per-sensor baselines | 3+ months of telemetry per sensor | Adaptive thresholds that reduce false positives vs static DMN rules |
| **Optimal Crew Dispatch** | Route optimisation + ETA prediction model | Crew GPS history, traffic data, job locations, skill matrix, repair time by type | Suggested crew assignment, predicted ETA, route |
| **Verification Confidence Prediction** | Regression model predicting verification success | before_flow, after_flow, confidence, crew_id, zone, season, repair_type | Predicted verification confidence before crew arrives |
| **Revenue Recovery Forecast** | Time-series + regression model | Recovered_volume history, tariff changes, seasonal patterns, active leaks | Forecast revenue by zone/month with confidence intervals |

## Technical Implications

To deliver the data product portfolio, the platform needs:

1. **Charting library** — Recharts, D3, or Apache ECharts (currently none)
2. **Analytics microservice** or **read-replica database** — separate OLAP concerns from OLTP to avoid performance impact
3. **Scheduled report engine** — cron-based or Celery-based PDF/CSV generation (can use existing Python stack)
4. **Materialised views** in PostgreSQL for common aggregations (daily zone stats, crew performance, revenue trends)
5. **Time-series rollups** in TimescaleDB — continuous aggregates for hourly/daily/weekly telemetry views (native TimescaleDB feature, currently unused)
6. **Data export endpoints** — CSV/JSON/PDF for every table and aggregation
7. **Clickable KPIs** — drill-down from summary cards to detail views (requires routing + state management)
8. **Anonymisation layer** for cross-tenant benchmarks (strip tenant_id, normalise by zone size/population)

**None of this requires new infrastructure.** PostgreSQL, TimescaleDB, and the existing Python/FastAPI stack can handle all Tier 1 products. The existing Kafka event bus already carries every data point needed.

## Strategic Narrative

### What METSI is today:
A **leak-to-revenue orchestration platform** that connects sensors, crews, contractors, municipalities, investors, and auditors in a single operational workflow.

### What METSI will become:
A **water intelligence utility** — the definitive source of water network performance data for Africa. Every leak repaired improves the model. Every municipality onboarded sharpens the benchmarks. Every year of telemetry increases the accuracy of predictions and the depth of insight.

The data products are not an add-on. They are the **durable competitive advantage** — the moat that turns a well-executed operational platform into an indispensable infrastructure asset.
